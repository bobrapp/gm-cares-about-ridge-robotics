This season · 2026–27

BIOGLOW: ideas for the Ridge Robotics playbook

The FIRST CANOPY season launched August 4, 2026. The FIRST LEGO League challenge is BIOGLOW, built around biodiversity: how technology, creativity and field research help us understand and protect living things.22 23 Challenge teams run a SPIKE Prime robot through two-and-a-half-minute matches and present an Innovation Project to judges.23 22 Grant County happens to be a biodiversity goldmine. A few starting points, offered as ideas, not assignments.

Innovation Project · local hook

Soap Lake literally glows

Five miles up the road, Soap Lake is one of the world's few meromictic lakes, with a mineral-rich bottom layer that hosts unusual microbial life. A team that studies what lives in Soap Lake, Moses Lake or the Potholes, and designs a way to protect or monitor it, is telling a BIOGLOW story no other team in the country can tell. Ask the Soap Lake Conservancy or WSU Extension for a guest expert.

Innovation Project

Pollinators for the orchards

Apples and cherries around Ephrata depend on bees. Ideas: a bee-hotel with a temperature sensor, a bloom-timing alert for growers, or a kid-built pollinator strip plan for the school grounds.

Innovation Project

Salmon and the Columbia

Fish passage, water temperature and side-channel habitat. A model of a smart fish counter or a cooling shade structure makes a great judging prop.

Innovation Project

Sage grouse and shrub-steppe

Beezley Hills is right above town. A trail camera triggered only by motion of a certain size, or a seed-planting robot for sagebrush restoration.

Innovation Project

Bats, the quiet pest control

Bats eat orchard pests every night. A bat-house with a sound logger shows how biodiversity pays farmers back.

Robot Game · what Engineering Excellence judges look for

Win on consistency, not ambition

  • Pick the missions you can complete three out of three times. A reliable 200 beats an occasional 300.
  • Group missions by mat zone into "runs," and design one quick-change attachment per run. Time the swaps; they are part of the 2:30.
  • Square the robot against walls and use gyro turns, so every run starts from the same place. Last year's color sensor trick was this kind of thinking.6 7
  • Keep an engineering notebook: what you tried, what broke, what you changed. Judges read it. It is how Engineering Excellence gets awarded.
  • Rehearse the judging room without adults, exactly like New Jersey. The team already knows how.13 14
Core Values · the Tiger way

Keep doing the thing judges keep noticing

Two awards in one season were for how the team treats people.5 12 Bring a small gift or a cheer for the team at the next table. Help a team whose robot broke. Write it down afterward: judges ask for examples.

Winning ways

How GM shows up for robotics, kids and the road ahead

GM Cares is not a slogan. It is a grassroots employee program that has been showing up in gyms and classrooms since 2011, and it sits on top of a company that helped found FIRST itself.

1.3M+ hours

GM Cares, by GM employees

Since 2011, GM employees have contributed more than 1.3 million volunteer hours and more than $16.3 million in direct donations to nonprofits through GM Cares, the company's global employee giving program.28 29 It grew out of teamGM Cares, a volunteer-led, company-wide employee initiative; in 2017 alone more than 18,000 GM employees logged 161,000 volunteer hours.30 31 32

Founding sponsor

GM and FIRST

GM is a Founding Sponsor of FIRST and sponsors or supports hundreds of teams; employees coach and mentor children ages 6 to 18 across all FIRST programs, including FIRST LEGO League.37 38 GM directly sponsors dozens of teams each season, supports hundreds more, and has reached more than 10,000 students through FIRST.38 39

1 billion

Hands-free miles on Super Cruise

GM customers have driven a billion hands-free miles on Super Cruise, across nearly 750,000 vehicles and 23 models, generating the real-world data that trains GM's AI models.40 41 It is the same loop a FLL team runs: drive, measure, fix, drive again.

2028

From hands-off to eyes-off

GM plans to launch eyes-off driving on highways starting with the Cadillac Escalade IQ in 2028, built on a new centralized computing architecture that scales from Cadillac to Chevrolet.42 47 CEO Mary Barra said in October 2026 the program remains on track.45 46

5M miles

What Cruise taught GM

Cruise logged more than 5 million fully driverless miles in some of the most complex cities in the country.42 44 GM wound down the robotaxi business in December 2024 and folded that work into autonomy for the cars people own,45 43 with more than 200 supervised test vehicles now on public highways in California and Michigan.45 42 40

Our work with kids in STEM

  • A World In Motion brings about 2,000 GM employees into K–12 classrooms for hands-on, team-based STEM activities.48 49
  • GM backed Girls Who Code with a $250,000 grant to open computer science to middle- and high-school girls from underserved communities.48 50
  • GM supplies Chevrolet Bolt EVs for the AutoDrive Challenge, where university teams build a full autonomous passenger vehicle.48 49
  • Through GM Cares, employees fund classroom projects on DonorsChoose, and GM has partnered with Disney Imagination Campus to support teachers.51 52
  • GM de México has supported FIRST Robotics for 20 years, importing fields and kits and sending GM mentors to every team.53 54

Why an AI architect cares about a fourth-grade robot

The Ridge Robotics color sensor that told water from electrolytes is perception. The run that squares against a wall before every mission is localization. The notebook of what broke and what changed is validation. Those are the three jobs of a self-driving stack. The kids are already doing the work; they just call it the Robot Game.

Super Cruise is a hands-free driver assistance technology, not a self-driving system; drivers must stay attentive. Eyes-off capability is planned for 2028 subject to development, validation and other factors.41 47 Views on this page are those of the volunteers, not official statements of General Motors.
